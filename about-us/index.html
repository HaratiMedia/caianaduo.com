Caiana Duo – A Sonic Journey Through Brazilian Rhythms and Beyond

Caiana Duo is where strings and skins meet in a vibrant dialogue between melody, rhythm, and emotion. Formed by Abdallah Harati on the 7-string guitar and João Araújo on pandeiro and hand percussion, the duo invites listeners on an immersive journey through the intricate landscape of Brazilian instrumental music.

Rooted in the traditions of Choro, Samba, and Brazilian jazz, Caiana Duo blends the precision of classical technique with the fire of improvisation. Their repertoire is a tapestry of original compositions and reimagined works by Brazilian legends such as Pixinguinha, João Pernambuco, Heitor Villa-Lobos, and Baden Powell – all delivered with passion, virtuosity, and a touch of humor.

More than a concert, each performance is a storytelling experience. From the melancholic history behind Pixinguinha's Lamentos to a humorous protest tune inspired by a poorly played shaker, the duo brings music to life with context, spirit, and authenticity.

Caiana Duo's unique arrangements push the boundaries of tradition while honoring its roots – whether transforming a classical cathedral piece into a danceable 6/8 groove, or giving a jazz standard like Take Five a tropical twist with syncopated pandeiro beats. Their music is both refined and spontaneous, crafted for those who seek sophistication without losing the pulse of the street.

With a deep respect for cultural heritage and a bold artistic voice, Caiana Duo builds bridges between the local and the universal, the playful and the profound. It's music that speaks to the heart, moves the body, and stirs the soul.

Welcome to the sound of Caiana – where rhythm tells stories.
